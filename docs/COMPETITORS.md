# COMPETITORS — what we copy, what we beat

Rule: **take their best idea, make it simpler; take their biggest weakness, make it our strength.**
(Feature notes are based on public information and common user feedback. Verify details before marketing claims.)

## 1. Old desktop leaders

### TallyPrime (accounting standard)
| Their best (we adopt) | Their weakness (we beat) |
|---|---|
| Rock-solid double-entry accounting, every CA knows it | Hard to learn, old screens |
| Keyboard-only fast entry, shortcuts everywhere | Weak mobile, weak cloud |
| Strong GST returns, e-invoice, e-way bill | No shop features (QR order, WhatsApp receipt, attendance) |
| Voucher types, ledgers, cost centres | Multi-user/remote access needs extra setup |
| Data security, audit trail | English-heavy, not shopkeeper friendly |
**We do:** Tally-grade ledger + **Tally XML export** so CAs are happy, with a modern simple UI.

### Marg ERP 9+ (pharma & distribution leader)
| Their best (we adopt) | Their weakness (we beat) |
|---|---|
| Batch, expiry, near-expiry, salt-wise search | Complex interface, steep learning |
| Scheme / free goods, party-wise rates | Many complaints about service/support |
| Distributor features: salesman, route, outstanding | Old look, heavy desktop |
| Retailer/salesman/owner mobile apps | Cloud and mobile feel bolted-on |
**We do:** Marg-level pharma pack, but clean UI + local on-site support.

### BUSY (SME accounting & inventory)
| Their best | Their weakness |
|---|---|
| Multi-godown, stock transfer, job work | Old UI, weak mobile/cloud |
| Good for distributors, configurable | Customization needs expert |
**We do:** multi-godown + transfer in core, no-code customization (designer).
